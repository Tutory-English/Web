Syllabus

Apa yang Akan Kamu Pelajari

10 sesi terstruktur mencakup keempat skill IELTS dari dasar hingga siap ujian.

# Main Focus IELTS Material Grammar / Language Focus
1 IELTS Foundation & Getting Ready IELTS test format & scoring; Understanding the 4 skills; Introduction to IELTS question types; Understanding what Band 5.5–6.5 requires Word Forms: noun / verb / adjective / adverb; singular & plural nouns; common spelling patterns; recognising grammatical clues before an answer
2 Listening: Getting Ready to Listen Understanding the context; identifying speakers & their purpose; following a conversation; form / note completion; predicting answers before listening Tenses for Listening: Present Simple; Past Simple; Present Perfect; Future forms; recognising tense changes while listening
3 Listening: Paraphrasing & Distractors Recognising paraphrases; identifying distractors; selecting information from a list; understanding changes/corrections in conversations; spelling, numbers & dates Sentence Structure: subject + verb + object; simple & compound sentences; sentence fragments & run-on sentences; basic word order
4 Reading: Skimming & Scanning Features of a Reading passage; skimming for main ideas; speed reading; global understanding; scanning for specific information; locating keywords Reference & Pronouns: it / they / this / these / such; understanding what a pronoun refers to; identifying relationships between sentences
5 Reading: Main Ideas & Matching Information Identifying the main idea; understanding main points; identifying information in a passage; matching information; understanding how ideas are connected Complex Sentences: because / although / while / whereas; relative clauses: who / which / that; understanding long sentences in Reading
6 Writing Task 2: Getting Ready to Write Understanding the question; identifying question types; planning & organising ideas; writing an introduction; writing a clear position/thesis Sentence Variety: simple vs. compound vs. complex sentences; coordinating conjunctions; subordinating conjunctions; avoiding repetitive sentence structures
7 Writing Task 2: Expressing Ideas Clearly Body paragraph structure; linking ideas; developing explanations; giving examples; avoiding repetition; expressing a personal view Cause & Effect + Modality: lead to / result in / cause; therefore / as a result; should / could / might / may; expressing certainty & possibility
8 Speaking Part 1 & Part 2 Getting ready to speak; Part 1: familiar topics; extending answers; Part 2: understanding the cue card; organising notes & ideas; improving fluency & coherence Tenses for Speaking: Present / Past / Present Perfect / Future; choosing the right tense; using different tenses in one answer
9 Speaking Part 3 + Language Upgrade Talking about abstract topics; giving opinions; agreeing & disagreeing; comparing ideas; explaining reasons & consequences; developing answers Conditionals & Comparison: Zero / First / Second Conditional; comparatives & superlatives; more/less likely to; the most / the least
10 IELTS Practice + Error Correction Mini IELTS practice; Reading & Listening strategy review; Writing response; Speaking practice; understanding common IELTS mistakes; personal improvement strategy Grammar Review & Accuracy: subject–verb agreement; articles; tenses; prepositions; sentence structure; identifying & correcting individual grammar mistakes
